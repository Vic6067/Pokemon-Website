THE WORLD OF COLLECTING

Welcome to Booster Mania

This website was created as a showcase of my own personal hobby of collecting and opening Pokemon booster packs. Here, you can find information about the prices of different booster packs, pull rates for various cards, and a rare distribution analysis. Additionally, I have an archive of my own personal collection and pulls that I have found. For me, Pokemon collecting was a way to bond with my boyfriend, but I got so into it that I began to collect my own and now I have a collection of over 1000 cards.

MORE THAN JUST CARDS

Why I Love Pokemon Collecting

Pokemon collecting is more than simply buying packs and hoping to find a rare card. It is about the excitement of discovering something new, appreciating the artwork, and slowly building a collection that feels personal.

Every booster pack brings a little bit of anticipation. You never really know what you are going to find inside, and that is one of the things that makes opening packs so enjoyable.

Over time, collecting has also become a way to create memories. Whether it is opening packs together, trading cards, or finding a card that has been on a wishlist for a long time, every little moment adds something special to the hobby.

THE EXCITEMENT OF OPENING

Exploring Booster Packs

Booster packs are one of the most exciting parts of collecting Pokemon cards. Each pack contains a selection of cards from a particular expansion, giving collectors the opportunity to discover new Pokemon, artwork, and rarities.

Every expansion has its own theme, artwork style, and collection of cards. Some sets become favourites because of their beautiful illustrations, while others stand out because of the cards that collectors are hoping to find.

What Makes Each Set Special?

From the design of the cards to the variety of available rarities, every Pokemon expansion has something different to offer. Exploring different sets is a great way to discover new favourites and expand a collection.

UNDERSTANDING THE CARDS

The Beauty of Rare Cards

One of the most interesting aspects of Pokemon collecting is the variety of card rarities. From standard cards to beautifully illustrated special cards, each rarity offers something different to collectors.

Rare cards often stand out because of their unique artwork, special finishes, textures, or limited availability within a set. Some become highly sought after, while others are appreciated simply because of their design.

I enjoy learning about the different rarities and discovering how cards are distributed throughout different expansions. This is also why I wanted to include pull rates and rare distribution information on this website.
